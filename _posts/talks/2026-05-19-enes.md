The European Network for Earth System Modelling (ENES) has spent more than two decades building collaboration and shared infrastructure to support European climate modelling. This talk explores the evolution of ENES into a sustained, distributed research infrastructure (ENES-RI), bringing together 17 European partner organisations to provide services spanning the full climate modelling lifecycle.

<span class="presentation-link">Presentation: [pdf](/assets/talks/2026-05-19-ENES.pdf) (3.2 MB)</span>

The ENES-RI services extend from shared model components, software tools and high-performance computing (HPC) support, through simulation workflows and data distribution, to model evaluation and scientific analysis. The talk considers how the infrastructure has evolved in response to increasing model complexity, growing data volumes, the transition towards exascale computing, and the expanding demands for actionable climate information.

It also introduces ENES-RISe, a new initiative to strengthen and extend these services, particularly in HPC adaptation, computational performance, workflow enhancement and access to infrastructure. Finally, it considers the challenges of sustaining and integrating this distributed European capability over the longer term.
