The original intention of this talk was to introduce how k-scale global modelling fitted into 
the UK National Climate Science Partnership's modelling strategy, but that wasn't quite
ready, so I introduced the NCAS strategy.

<span class="presentation-link">Presentation: [pdf](/assets/talks/2026-07-23-kscale.pdf) (8 MB)</span>

I did that in the context of a wee look at what the CANARI large ensemble says about what
we might see as a maximum temperature during UK summers, and how much variability there
is between simulations (and linked that back to an intro to variability in climate modelling).
The key take home was that without large ensembles, we can't have confidence in what k-scale
models tell us.
