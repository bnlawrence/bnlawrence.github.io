In this talk I introduced the CANARI large ensemble to a general physics audience.

<span class="presentation-link">Presentation: [pdf](/assets/talks/2025-02-14-canari-talk.pdf) (16.4 MB)</span>

The talk started with an introduction to numerical models and concepts of resolution, before explaining how large models use large compute. I introduced some ideas of
uncertainty and storylines, before introducing ensembles in general and large ensembles specifically. I discussed the [CANARI](https://canari.ac.uk) project itself,
and then the CANARI model and ensemble configuraion (including boundary condition
production for CORDEX and other users). I discussed the reality of keeping
a large ensemble in the machine, and the volumes of data (6 PB) produced.

I then introduced some of the early science results from colleagues, including some analysis of cyclone tracks and European storms.
