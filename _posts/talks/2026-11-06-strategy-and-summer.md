This seminar introduced the current nearly final draft NCAS climate modelling
strategy and showed how we use large-ensembles to motivate the link between
high resolution and earth system modelling, using a UK summer example.

<span class="presentation-link">Presentation: [pdf](/assets/talks/2026-11-06-ncas-summer.pdf) (15.2 MB)</span>

The NCAS climate modelling programme delivers national strategic objectives in climate modelling.  In a context where science and policy can be in tension around delivering results and delivering understanding, from global to regional scales, there can be no one single model solution.  Here we present the NCAS climate model strategy, addressing what is happening now, and where we are going next. The current review includes a quick summary of some recent activities, and a look at what the CANARI large ensemble tells us about the future of hot summers in the UK.  
The future look covers plans for large scale modelling and some of the various technology options being explored.
